Auth Flow Concepts — Viluma

Discussion Mockup · April 2026

Three pattern options to evaluate. Each trades off friction, trust, and technical complexity differently. Pick a direction or mix-and-match parts.

Pattern A — Email-First, One Modal (Airbnb model)

Step 1 · Enter email

Welcome to Viluma

Sign in or create an account

or

By continuing, you agree to our Terms and Privacy Policy.

One screen handles both sign-in and sign-up — system decides after email lookup. Social logins above the fold.

Step 2a · Returning user

Welcome back, Sofia

Signing in as sofia@example.com

Forgot password? · Use a magic link instead

No confirm-password field — removing it lifted conversion ~56% in studies. Magic link as escape hatch.

Step 2b · New user

Create your account

One more thing and you're in.

We'll never post or share without your permission.

Minimal fields — only what's needed to personalise. Profile details (interests, location) deferred to onboarding.

Pattern B — Passwordless / Magic Link First

Step 1 · Request link

Sign in to Viluma

We'll email you a secure link — no password needed.

or sign in with

Link expires in 15 min. Set a password instead

Lowest friction for new users. Removes password anxiety entirely — fits wellness/mindful brand voice well.

+ no password management − email-switch friction on mobile
Step 2 · Email sent
✉️

Check your inbox

We sent a link to sofia@example.com.
Click it to sign in instantly.

Resend link

Dead-simple confirmation. Timer or "open Gmail" deeplink helps on mobile.

Step 3 · Progressive profiling (new users only)

What draws you here?

We'll personalise your experience.

Yoga retreats Meditation Hiking Surf camps Sound healing Digital detox

Skip for now

Deferred profiling — collected after auth, not before. Improves feed personalisation without blocking signup.

Recommendation summary
My pick for Viluma

Pattern A + C hybrid

Email-first modal on desktop → bottom sheet on mobile. Progressive profiling (interests) after first login. Magic link as opt-in alternative, not default.

✓

Google/Apple social first — highest-converting path, especially for new users discovering via search

✓

Email-first means one screen handles both flows — no "login vs. register" decision for users

✓

Defer profiling — collect interests after auth, shown once, skippable

△

Magic link as fallback (not default) — reduces email-switching friction on mobile while keeping it available

What to skip: OTP/SMS (adds cost + latency), separate login/register pages (doubles surface area), confirm-password field (kills conversion).
