How to choose a training

Most training-seekers commit to a discipline first, certification level second. If you know you want to become a yoga teacher, start by choosing your style and lineage — Vinyasa, Hatha, Yin, Kundalini, Ashtanga — then choose 200hr (entry credential) or 300hr/500hr (advanced). If you're called toward a different discipline — breathwork, sound healing, somatic therapy, coaching — start from the trainings search and filter by discipline, then by hours, then by start window. The hero search above mirrors that flow.

Yoga Teacher Training — 200hr, 300hr, 500hr

The 200hr Yoga Teacher Training is the entry credential for becoming a yoga teacher and meets the Yoga Alliance RYT-200 standard. Programs range from 3-week intensives in Bali, Rishikesh, or Costa Rica (typically €1,400–€3,500 with accommodation included) to 9-month modular trainings in Berlin, Amsterdam or London where you train on weekends while keeping your day job (typically €2,500–€3,500 tuition only). The 300hr is the advanced upgrade (RYT-500 when combined with 200hr), and the 500hr is offered as an integrated master program by senior schools.

Lumaya filters your results by hours, format (intensive vs modular vs online), country, accreditation body and start month — so you can compare a 200hr in Bali starting October with a modular 200hr in your home city starting September against the same five fields.

Frequently asked questions

What is a 200hr Yoga Teacher Training?

A 200hr Yoga Teacher Training (200hr YTT) is the entry-level qualification to teach yoga professionally, meeting the Yoga Alliance RYT-200 standard. The 200 hours cover technique, teaching methodology, anatomy, philosophy, ethics and practicum. Programs run as 3–4 week intensives or 6–9 month modular trainings.

What's the difference between 200hr, 300hr and 500hr YTT?

The 200hr is your entry credential. The 300hr is an advanced training you complete after 200hr — combined, they qualify you as RYT-500. Some schools offer integrated 500hr master trainings that cover both in a single program. 500hr graduates typically have deeper command of anatomy, advanced asana, sequencing and teaching methodology.

How much does a yoga teacher training cost?

Tuition ranges from around €1,400 for a 200hr in Rishikesh (with accommodation included) to €3,500–€4,500 for a 200hr in Berlin or Bali. 300hr trainings run €2,500–€4,500. 500hr master programs and 3-year SE programs reach €5,000–€8,000+. Modular trainings are usually tuition-only; residential intensives include accommodation and meals.

Do I need to be advanced to take a 200hr YTT?

No. Most 200hr programs are designed for committed practitioners with at least 1–2 years of regular practice. The training itself teaches you to teach — you don't need to be able to do every advanced posture. Schools are clear about prerequisites, and Lumaya shows them on every listing.

Are online trainings recognized by Yoga Alliance?

Some are — Yoga Alliance temporarily approved fully online RYTs during 2020–2021 and now allows hybrid programs (mostly in-person with limited online components). Fully online "200hr in 30 days" programs from unaccredited providers are not recognized. Lumaya lists each program's accreditation status clearly.
