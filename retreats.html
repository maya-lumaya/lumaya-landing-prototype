How to choose a retreat

Most retreat-seekers commit to a practice first, destination second — and that's how Lumaya is structured. If you already know you want a vinyasa yoga retreat, a silent meditation retreat or an Ayurveda detox, start at the retreats search and choose your modality. If you're flexible on practice but committed to a region — Bali, Rishikesh, Portugal, Costa Rica — start from a destination chip instead. Either path lands you in the same filtered results page, where you can layer on length (weekend, short 4–7 nights, week-plus, deep two-weeks-plus) and season (Spring 2026, Summer 2026, Autumn 2026, Winter 2026/27).

We separate these from "wellness theatre" deliberately.

Frequently asked questions

What is a yoga retreat?

A yoga retreat is a multi-day residential programme combining daily asana practice, meditation, pranayama, healthy meals and rest, usually held away from home in a quiet or natural setting. Programmes range from 3-night long-weekends to 28-day Yoga Teacher Trainings. Most retreats include accommodation, meals and the full schedule of classes in a single fee.

How much does a yoga retreat cost?

Retreats on Lumaya start from around €480 for a 3-night breathwork weekend and reach €5,000+ for 28-day teacher-training intensives. The mid-range — a 7-night yoga retreat in Bali, Mallorca or Costa Rica with shared accommodation — typically falls between €840 and €1,200 including all classes, meals and accommodation.

What's the difference between a retreat, a training and an event?

An event is a single session or evening class — a 90-minute kirtan, a Sunday cacao ceremony, a 6-week breathwork course. A retreat is multi-day, residential, immersive and usually destination-based. A training is a credentialed certification programme — 200hr Yoga Teacher Training, MBSR Certification, breathwork facilitator training — that prepares you to teach. Lumaya offers all three as separate pillars: Events, Retreats, and Trainings.

Can I book a retreat without paying upfront?

Most retreats require a deposit (typically €100–€500) at booking, with the balance due 60–90 days before arrival. Some hosts offer payment plans. Donation-based retreats (some Vipassana centres) are paid after the retreat at your discretion. Refund and cancellation policies are set by the host and displayed clearly on each retreat listing.

Are retreats safe for beginners?

Yes — most retreats welcome beginners, and listings clearly indicate experience level. Beginner-friendly categories include Yoga for Beginners, mindfulness retreats and most wellness/Ayurveda programmes. More demanding containers (10-day Vipassana, 200hr YTT, dark retreats, plant-medicine sittings) are best entered after some practice or with adequate preparation, and the host's listing will note this.
