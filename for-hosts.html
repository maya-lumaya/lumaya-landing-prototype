Pricing

Simple and fair

We believe in keeping the barrier low. List your events for free — always. Retreats and trainings listed before the end of 2026 are on us, too.

Events
Free
List your regular events, workshops, and classes at no cost. No catch, no time limit. This is how it stays.
Retreats & Trainings
Free until 2027
List retreats and multi-day trainings for free through the end of 2026. After that, a small commission covers our running costs — nothing more.

We are not building a profit machine. Viluma runs on a commission-based model designed to be as low as possible — just enough to keep the platform running and growing. No subscription fees, no hidden costs.
