Conscious Events — Yoga, Breathwork, Dance & Ceremony Across Europe

Conscious events have grown from niche weekend retreats into a living weekly calendar spread across dozens of European cities. What began in the 1990s as underground practices — holotropic breathing in converted warehouses, 5Rhythms dance in community halls, Vipassana retreats far from the city — has matured into a thriving ecosystem of studios, ceremony spaces, and community-led gatherings that run seven days a week across Berlin, Vienna, London, Amsterdam, Lisbon, Barcelona, Zurich, and beyond. Viluma was built to make this ecosystem visible in one place.

What makes an event "conscious"?

The term is intentionally broad, but it always implies a quality of presence — both from the facilitator and the participant. Yoga events on Viluma range from early-morning led flows to deep yin and restorative practices taught by senior teachers with decades in their lineage. Breathwork events span pranayama circles, holotropic intensives, and the newer wave of Clarity Breathwork and Transformational Breath sessions. Ecstatic dance, 5Rhythms, Soul Motion, and Open Floor gatherings give the body a floor to process what words can't. Cacao ceremonies, sound baths, and kirtan use rhythm, plant medicine, and vibration to create group altered states without losing safety or intention. Somatic practices, tantra workshops, and conscious connection events address the body, intimacy, and nervous system regulation in ways that mainstream fitness culture simply cannot.

How Viluma curates its events

Every event on Viluma is submitted by a host who holds a verified profile. We don't auto-import from ticket platforms or aggregate raw listings. Hosts describe their practice, their lineage, and the container they hold. This means you know — before you book — whether you're entering a highly structured Tibetan Buddhist session or a loose, playful cacao circle with open-floor dance to follow. Authenticity over volume is the founding constraint that shapes everything else on the platform.

Finding events near you

Use the search bar above to find events in your city or neighbourhood. You can filter by practice type, date, and distance. Regional hubs like our Berlin page give you a curated window into a single city's conscious-event scene — the hosts, the neighbourhoods, the recurring series, and the one-off ceremonies that happen too fast for most search engines to catch. If you're travelling, the Online (Live) hub gathers events that cross borders: guided meditations, breathwork, and somatic workshops that work just as well over a solid internet connection.

For hosts and organisers

Viluma is designed around practitioners, not algorithms. If you run events, hold space, or facilitate gatherings that belong in this ecosystem, join Viluma as a host. Your profile sits alongside your events, your story reaches people who are genuinely looking for what you offer, and booking comes direct — no platform intermediary taking a cut of your trust-based work. The community here is ready for what you're building.
